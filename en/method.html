The 60-second anti-scam method

🕵️

The “almost perfect” fake site

A fraudulent page can look polished. Try About, Contact, FAQ or the logo: some fake sites copy only one page. But a complete site can still be fake. Checking the exact domain name remains more important.

1. Stop
Do not respond under pressure. A real account issue can be checked without following the received link.
2. Read the address
Check the exact domain carefully; one changed character can mean a different site.
3. Test, but do not trust
About / Contact / FAQ can expose an incomplete clone. It is a clue, not proof.
4. Close it
If in doubt, close the page even if it looks genuine.
5. Start fresh
Open the official app yourself or type the official address. Do not return through the message.
6. Confirm
If the alert is absent from your official account, treat the message as suspicious.
⭐

The golden rule

If a message sends you to a link, do not keep browsing from that link. Close it, open the official website or app yourself, and check your account there.
